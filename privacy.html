This Privacy Policy explains how MF-CFO ("MF-CFO", "we", "us") collects and uses information in connection with text messages sent from and received at (480) 418-6096.

Information we collect

  • Your name and mobile phone number, which you provide when you give MF-CFO verbal consent to receive texts.

How we use it

We use this information only to send and answer conversational text messages about scheduling, project and job updates, and questions between you and MF-CFO, and to keep a record of your consent and any opt-out request.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Text message consent and phone numbers are not shared with any third party. Messages are carried by our SMS provider (Twilio) solely to deliver them.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help. After you reply STOP, you will receive one confirmation and no further messages unless you opt in again.

Retention and security

We keep consent records and message history only as long as needed for the purposes above, and we protect them with reasonable safeguards.
