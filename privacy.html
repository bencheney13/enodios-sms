MF-CFO Privacy Policy

MF-CFO · Scottsdale, Arizona · SMS (480) 418-6096 · ben@mf-cfo.com · Effective October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MF-CFO ("MF-CFO", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects information in connection with the MF-CFO Project Updates text messaging program at (480) 418-6096.

Information we collect

  • Your name and mobile phone number, which you give us when you consent to receive texts.
  • The date, time and method of your consent.
  • The content of text messages you send to or receive from (480) 418-6096.

How we use it

We use this information only to send and answer text messages about scheduling, deliveries, inspections, project updates and questions on work MF-CFO is doing with you, and to keep a record of your consent and any opt-out request.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help. After you reply STOP you will receive one confirmation and no further messages unless you opt in again.

Retention and security

We keep consent records and message history only as long as needed for the purposes above and protect them with reasonable administrative and technical safeguards.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above shows the latest version.

Contact

MF-CFO, Scottsdale, Arizona. Email ben@mf-cfo.com, or text HELP to (480) 418-6096.
