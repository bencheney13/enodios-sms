MF-CFO · Scottsdale, Arizona · SMS (480) 418-6096 · ben@mf-cfo.com · Effective October 8, 2026

MF-CFO texts only people it works with directly: its clients and the subcontractors and suppliers on client projects, and only after they agree. Consent is collected verbally, in person or by phone.

Verbal consent script

Before any text is sent, MF-CFO reads this disclosure and asks for a yes:

"Can MF-CFO text you from (480) 418-6096 about scheduling, project updates and documents for the work we're doing together? Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help and STOP to opt out. Our terms and privacy policy are at bencheney13.github.io/enodios-sms. Is that OK?"

Only people who say yes are added. MF-CFO records each person's name, mobile number, the date and time, and that consent was given verbally. The first text confirms the sign-up:

MF-CFO: You're signed up for texts from MF-CFO about scheduling, project updates and documents. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP at any time; you will get one confirmation and no further texts. If you opted out and want texts again, text START to (480) 418-6096. Reply HELP or email ben@mf-cfo.com for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Phone numbers are never bought, shared or used for marketing.
