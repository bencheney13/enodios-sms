Enodios BDC Agent

MF-CFO · private internal tool · Effective October 8, 2026

Enodios BDC Agent is the private connector MF-CFO uses to read and keep the QuickBooks Online books of MF-CFO's own companies and of the clients it does accounting work for. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

Using it

The connector runs on MF-CFO's own computer. There is nothing to open here. A company is connected by MF-CFO signing in to QuickBooks and approving the connection for that company.

Disconnecting

A company can be disconnected at any time, by either side:

  • In QuickBooks Online: Apps, then My Apps, then Disconnect on Enodios BDC Agent.
  • By asking MF-CFO, which revokes the connection and deletes that company's stored sign-in.

After a disconnect the connector can no longer reach that company's books.
