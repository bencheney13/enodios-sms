Enodios BDC Agent: Privacy Policy

MF-CFO · private internal tool · Effective October 8, 2026

This policy explains how MF-CFO ("MF-CFO", "we") handles information reached through Enodios BDC Agent ("the connector"), a private QuickBooks Online connector used only by MF-CFO.

Information the connector reaches

For each QuickBooks Online company connected to it, the connector can read and record that company's accounting data: accounts, customers, vendors, transactions and reports.

How we use it

We use it only to do the accounting and bookkeeping work MF-CFO performs for that company. MF-CFO uses AI software as an assistant in that work, under MF-CFO's direction and review.

Sharing

We do not sell this information, and we do not share it for marketing. It is not used to train AI models. It is handled by MF-CFO and by the service providers MF-CFO relies on to do the work, and disclosed otherwise only when the company's owner asks or the law requires.

What is stored

The connector stores a sign-in token for each connected company on MF-CFO's own computer, with access limited to MF-CFO. It keeps a log of the changes it makes (time, company and record type), not the accounting entries themselves. Working papers MF-CFO prepares are kept in MF-CFO's own files.

Ending a connection

A company's owner may disconnect the connector at any time; see the disconnect steps. On disconnect, MF-CFO deletes that company's stored sign-in token.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact MF-CFO directly.
