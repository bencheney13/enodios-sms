MF-CFO Project Updates: How to Opt In

MF-CFO · Scottsdale, Arizona · SMS (480) 418-6096 · ben@mf-cfo.com · Effective October 8, 2026

MF-CFO texts only people it works with directly on active projects (clients, subcontractors, suppliers and project partners), and only after they agree. There are two ways to opt in.

1. Verbal consent (in person or by phone)

Before any text is sent, MF-CFO reads this disclosure and asks for a yes:

"Can MF-CFO text you from (480) 418-6096 about scheduling, deliveries, inspections and project updates? Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Our privacy policy and terms are at bencheney13.github.io/enodios-sms. Is that OK?"

Only people who say yes are added. MF-CFO records each person's name, mobile number, the date and time, and that consent was given verbally. The first text confirms the sign-up:

MF-CFO: You're signed up for MF-CFO Project Updates about scheduling and project updates. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

2. Text to opt in

Text START to (480) 418-6096. You will receive the confirmation message above.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP at any time; you will get one confirmation and no further texts. Reply HELP or email ben@mf-cfo.com for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Phone numbers are never bought, shared or used for marketing.
